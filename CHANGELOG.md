## [Unreleased]

### Added

The first public release.

- A procedural island: terrain, grass that bends in the wind, forests of maple, ginkgo, pine, cedar and bamboo, water, sky and a mountain backdrop, and the architecture standing on it (torii, a shrine and its stairs, a pagoda, a farmhouse and a bandit camp). All of it is generated in code, with no art assets.
- Third-person samurai combat: light and heavy attacks, parry, dodge and stand-off duels against bandits.
- A mountable horse: call it with a whistle, mount it and ride it at a walk, trot or gallop.
- Wildlife: crows, sparrows and a kite in the air, a fox and sika deer on the ground.
- Procedural audio and music: sound effects, ambience and music synthesized in real time with the Web Audio API.
- An ink-brush interface: title screen, HUD, a pause menu that holds the map, and a photo mode. The text is available in English and Italian, and the settings are saved in the browser.
- A unit-test suite built on `node:test`, run with `npm test`.
- Developer capture tools, driven from npm scripts: screenshot captures, and a trot lab that measures the horse's gait.
